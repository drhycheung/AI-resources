## 🌟 Introduction

This Markdown file is an annotated list of generative AI (GenAI) tools that I use for teaching, learning, and research at the Education University of Hong Kong (EdUHK) in Hong Kong, China. It is written for my students, colleagues, and the general public who may be interested in using GenAI for these purposes.

A tool is included here only if:

1. I have used it;
2. It is accessible (in Hong Kong), stable, and useful; and
3. It has a not-too-restrictive free plan so that my students can use it for free in their learning.

If you know of any other useful tools that satisfy criteria 2 and 3, feel free to send me an email. I will consider including them after my evaluation.

### Ethical and Learning Issues

⚠️ **Avoid plagiarism and over-reliance**: For my students intending to use these GenAI tools for academic work:

1. While the University encourages the use of GenAI tools for your learning, assessment of your work will primarily be based on the original content contributed by you, rather than on any AI-generated content or other sources used. While the use of such content may be helpful in generating ideas or providing additional insights, it is your unique contribution and analysis that are most important in demonstrating your understanding and expertise in the field.
2. Remember that reading and writing are not only mechanical processes, but also cognitive ones. They involve learning and thinking, which are essential for our personal growth and development. Over-reliance on AI could be destructive to our development of critical thinking and problem-solving skills. These are the tools I would suggest trying first if you are new to GenAI or want a practical set of tools that cover the main tasks in teaching, learning, and research.

1. [(Link)](https://gemini.google.com/) **Gemini 📌**: the most practical general chatbot for everyday use, especially for multimodal work and custom chatbot workflows.
2. [(Link)](https://opencode.ai/) **OpenCode 📌**: a practical coding agent with a generous free tier for rapid prototyping and everyday development.
3. [(Link)](https://www.doubao.com/) **豆包 📌**: one of the most capable Chinese-language assistants for daily work, multimodal use, and quick practical help.
4. [(Link)](https://metaso.cn/) **秘塔 📌**: a useful search and assistant tool for research, reading, and knowledge discovery in Chinese and English contexts.
5. [(Link)](https://metaso.cn/echo) **秘塔回响 📌**: a highly practical voice-input tool for speaking in English or Chinese and capturing ideas quickly without typing.

### General Purpose Chatbots

These are the general-purpose AI assistants I use most often for daily tasks, conversation, and information retrieval.

1. [(Link)](https://gemini.google.com/) **Gemini 📌**: The most practical AI assistant for daily use now, especially for custom chatbots and multimodal work. Poe's generous free credits have dropped sharply, and Doubao has removed its custom agent feature, so Gemini's GEM tool is a strong replacement for a lot of everyday use.
2. [(Link)](https://genai.eduhk.hk/) **GenAI at EdUHK**: This GenAI service indicates the University's attitude to encourage the use of generative AI in teaching, learning, and research. For EdUHK staff and students only (VPN is needed if accessing off-campus.)
3. [(Link)](https://www.perplexity.ai) **Perplexity.ai**: A search-first AI assistant that answers with citations and concise web-grounded summaries.
4. [(Link)](https://poe.com/) **Poe**: Poe remains a highly usable chatbot with multiple bots, free access in Hong Kong, document upload, custom chatbots, conversations, web search, image generation, and multimodal support.

### AI Agents

These tools go beyond simple chat and can help with execution, coding, and more structured workflows.

1. [(Link)](https://claude.ai/code) **Claude Code**: I have also tried Claude Code, but it is too expensive for regular use in my workflow.
2. [(Link)](https://openai.com/codex) **Codex**: A capable coding agent worth trying for code generation and software task execution.
3. [(Link)](https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/) **Hermes Agent 📌**: Hermes Agent is designed for agentic workflows that span browsing, tool use, scheduling, memory, and delegation. It is especially interesting for people who want a persistent, multi-step assistant rather than a single-turn chatbot.
4. [(Link)](https://openclaw.ai/) **OpenClaw**: Another useful agent option for experimentation and programming assistance, especially if you want to explore beyond mainstream tools.
5. [(Link)](https://opencode.ai/) **OpenCode 📌**: This is a practical coding agent with a generous free tier, making it useful for rapid prototyping and everyday development tasks.
6. [(Link)](https://www.doubao.com/) **豆包工作任务 📌**: Doubao's agent-driven task mode (now evolving into 豆包工作), which understands goals, breaks tasks into steps, calls tools and connectors, and delivers finished work products. It supports skills, multi-agent "work companions", and scheduled/periodic tasks for everyday office work.
7. [(Link)](https://www.workbuddy.ai) **腾讯 WorkBuddy**: Tencent's all-scenario AI office workbench. Describe a task in plain language and it autonomously plans, executes, and delivers usable results—handling documents, spreadsheets, PPTs, data analysis, and local file operations, with 100+ domain experts, multi-agent teamwork, and a cloud 7×24 assistant.
